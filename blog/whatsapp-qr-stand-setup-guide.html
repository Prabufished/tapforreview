WhatsApp QR Stand Setup Guide

A WhatsApp QR stand opens a chat with your business number without asking customers to save a contact or type a number.

Choose the first message

Use a short, useful pre-filled message such as “Hi, I’d like to know more about your services.” This gives your team immediate context.

Place the stand where questions arise

Reception desks, display counters and takeaway areas work well. Make sure your staff knows who handles incoming chats.

Respond quickly

Set expectations and keep business hours current. A fast, helpful reply turns a scan into a real lead.
