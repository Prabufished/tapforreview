How to Get More Google Reviews for Your Salon

Ask after a positive appointment, while the experience is still fresh. Make the request optional, polite and easy to complete.

Use a simple moment

Place an NFC and QR stand near the billing counter. Your team can say: “If you were happy with today’s service, a Google review would really help us.”

Keep reviews authentic

Do not offer incentives for positive reviews or pressure customers. Ask every customer consistently and let their real experience guide what they write.

Reply to every review

Thank customers for positive feedback and respond calmly to concerns. A thoughtful reply shows potential clients that you care.
