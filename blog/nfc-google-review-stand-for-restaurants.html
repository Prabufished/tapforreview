Restaurant marketing guide

NFC Google Review Stand for Restaurants

Restaurant reviews are most likely to be completed while guests are still at the table or paying their bill. A visible NFC and QR stand removes the need to search for your listing.

Place it at the right moment

Use the billing counter, host desk or takeaway pickup point. Keep the message friendly and never interrupt the dining experience.

Give diners a choice

NFC works for compatible phones, while a QR code gives every customer another way to open the same review link.

Build trust over time

Focus on consistent service and genuine responses, not one-off review pushes.
